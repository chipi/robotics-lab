Questions for you

  1. Q1Mission Control's robot page now gives two verdicts side by side (#9, #25). The header and "Why it is fit to go" say Fit to go (5 of 5). The new Readiness card says check first (8 of 9), because the sim robot declares no guardrails. Five checks appear on both cards. Choose one: (a) Readiness replaces the fit card, and the header follows Readiness; (b) both stay, and Readiness keeps only its extra checks (link, battery, guardrails, settings); (c) leave both as they are.
  2. Q2The two cards treat battery differently. Readiness calls under 20 % an error, using the line where the server raises low_battery. The fit card says "its threshold comes with B2". Use 20 % in both, or another figure?
  3. Q3In Mission Control's palette, STOP is the first row and is selected when the palette opens (#23, #24). Ctrl+K and then Enter stops the robot, with no confirmation, as the plan asked. Keep that, or keep STOP first but select nothing until you type or press an arrow key?
  4. Q4Signal threshold bands (#15) appear only on a joint signal in a run that has no pose. That is because the Signals view opens only for runs without a pose, and a run's signals carry no limits of their own, so a real duck run shows none today. Choose: (a) let the Signals view open for runs with a pose; (b) add limits to a signal's declaration (a contract change); (c) both; (d) leave it.
  5. Q5The Observatory's top bar has a Live or Replay badge with "Return to live" (#1, #5). The old "Follow live" checkbox is still there and does the same job. Fold it into the badge in U26? A test that clicks the checkbox would change with it.
  6. Q6Anything else: say the number and what you would change.
